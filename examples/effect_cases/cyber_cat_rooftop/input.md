# Cyber Cat Rooftop Chase

## Idea

A cyber cat chases a glowing drone across a rainy Shanghai rooftop at night.

## Chinese Idea

一只赛博猫在雨夜上海屋顶追逐发光无人机。

## Demo Goal

This case is designed to test whether ShotForge can turn a concrete video brief
into visible physical targets, detect missing elements after generation, create a
targeted revision plan, and compare the first and second generated versions.
